Tutorials

One of SWAT4HCLS’s three pillars, every edition

Each SWAT4HCLS edition runs on three pillars: a tutorials day, a two-day conference, and a full biohackathon day (with a satellite hacking room running alongside the tutorials and conference days too — see Biohackathon). The tutorials day opens the event: half-day sessions run by at least one on-site facilitator, covering tools, standards, and methods relevant to semantic web applications in healthcare and life sciences.

Anyone can propose a tutorial through that year’s call for papers — proposals are reviewed alongside paper submissions.

Looking for this year’s tutorials — schedule, proposals, deadlines? See Basel 2027 Tutorials.
