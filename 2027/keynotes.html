Prof. Melissa Haendel

Sarah Graham Kenan Distinguished Professor, Department of Genetics & Director, Precision Health & Translational Informatics, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill · Co-founder, Monarch Initiative

[Keynote title to be announced]

[Abstract to be announced]

Dr. Haendel’s background is in molecular genetics and translational informatics. Her vision is to weave together health care systems, basic science research, and patient-generated data through the development of data integration technologies and innovative data capture strategies. Her research has focused on integration of genotype–phenotype data to improve rare disease diagnosis and mechanism discovery. She has contributed to international standards organizations worldwide, in particular the Mondo disease ontology, which reconciles and classifies global genetic and rare disease knowledge.
