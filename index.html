About the community

SWAT4HCLS began in 2008 as a single-day workshop and has run every year since (barring a pause around 2020–2021), rotating between host cities and volunteer local organizers. Since 2015 it has run as a multi-day event: a tutorial day, a two-day conference, and a hackathon day.

This site is split in two: the pages here cover the community and everything that spans editions — the full paper archive, who’s who, open positions. Programme, registration, and other details specific to the current conference live under Basel 2027.
