Welcome to SWAT4HCLS 2027: Bridging Life Sciences and Technology

We are thrilled to announce that for the second time, SWAT4HCLS is going to Basel, Switzerland! We are excited to welcome you all for what promises to be an inspiring and productive event.

Stay tuned for updates on this page and our social media channels. We encourage you to follow us and engage with our community. If you have suggestions for improvements, would like to (co-)organize a side event, or want to support the event through sponsorship and/or other means, please don’t hesitate to reach out. Your input and support are invaluable to us.

As in previous editions, we will have a day dedicated to tutorials and a biohackathon. We welcome suggestions and proposals for both.

We will soon announce the set of submission deadlines, so keep an eye out for those details.

We look forward to seeing you in Basel!
