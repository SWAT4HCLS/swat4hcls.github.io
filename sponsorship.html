Sponsorship

18th International SWAT4HCLS Conference · 1–4 February 2027 · Basel, Switzerland

SWAT4HCLS is run by a non-profit organization and relies on the voluntary work of organizers and its programme committee.

We offer predefined sponsorship packages, and are open to discuss a tailored package or alternative marketing strategies. The conference welcomes technology providers and data economy stakeholders, and organizers encourage creative ways to facilitate a link between these groups through sponsorship arrangements.

Interested in sponsoring? Email swat4hcls@gmail.com with “Sponsorship” as the subject line.
