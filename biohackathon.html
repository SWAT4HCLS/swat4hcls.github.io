Biohackathon

One of SWAT4HCLS’s three pillars, every edition

Each SWAT4HCLS edition runs on three pillars: a tutorials day, a two-day conference, and a full biohackathon day. A satellite hacking room also runs alongside the tutorials and conference days, so participants can start meeting and hacking early — the full hackathon day is where that work continues and comes together, not where it starts.

“Hackathon” undersells it. The SWAT4HCLS biohackathon isn’t just product development — it’s a place to start a new project, pitch a joint project proposal, draft a joint paper, or simply hack on something with people you just met. It aligns with the DBCLS BioHackathon format.

SWAT4HCLS’s biohackathon is also part of a wider network of biohackathons — DBCLS’s own event in Japan, BioHackathon Europe, and others — where the same projects keep showing up, carried forward by the same people. A single event generates a lot of energy and real intermediate results, but that momentum easily dissolves once everyone returns to their day jobs. Chaining participation across successive biohackathons is what actually lets a project progress — slowly, but steadily.
